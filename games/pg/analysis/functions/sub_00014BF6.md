(`analysis/rendering.md`). It then calls `sub_000481AB(index)` (present) for the
same four indices in order. Reads and writes no other globals. It is called twice
in the Poland route: at 31 s (the first compose) and at 38 s (an identical
redraw, no pixel changes).
